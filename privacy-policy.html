Privacy Policy

App: Dungeon Pixel Survivor
Effective date: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the mobile game Dungeon Pixel Survivor ("the App"), developed by Aiden ("we", "us"). The App does not provide a separate developer account system. On supported platforms, you may use a platform gaming account (Google Play Games or Apple Game Center/iCloud) for gaming and cloud-save features.

1. Information Collected

We do not collect personal data directly. However, the following third-party services (advertising and payments) may automatically collect, process, and share certain data to provide their functions, measure performance, prevent fraud, maintain security, and comply with law:

  • Device or other identifiers — including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and related device identifiers.
  • Approximate location — estimated from the device IP address; the App does not request precise location.
  • App activity and ad interactions — such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, ad clicks, and video views.
  • App information and performance — including crash logs, diagnostics, launch time, hang rate, energy usage, and SDK performance data.
  • Purchase history — if you choose to buy the optional in-app product ("Remove Ads"), Google Play Billing processes the purchase and the App receives the purchase status to unlock that feature. We do not receive or store payment method details such as card numbers.
  • Platform gaming identity — on Android, Google Play Games Services may process a player ID, gamertag, avatar, and sign-in status to authenticate the player and provide Play Games features.
  • Game progress — save data such as gold, unlocked characters, equipment, settings, and progression may be stored using Google Play Games Saved Games on Android or iCloud on iOS when the corresponding platform service is available.

Advertising-related data may be used and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance. Purchase history is used only to provide the purchased feature and for fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance. Data transmitted by the integrated SDKs is encrypted in transit.

2. Game Progress (Save Data)

Game save data is stored on your device. When the corresponding platform cloud service is available, it is also transmitted in encrypted form and stored through Google Play Games Saved Games on Android or iCloud on iOS so that progress can be restored or synchronized. Android and iOS use separate platform cloud services and save data is not transferred between them.

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and control ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path can vary by device). Purchasing the "Remove Ads" product stops in-app ads. The App does not provide user accounts. Local game progress can be deleted by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Cloud data and platform gaming profile information can be managed or deleted through your Google Play Games or Apple account settings. You may also contact us using the address below for assistance with a deletion request.

  • EEA / UK (GDPR): Where applicable, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Ad personalization in these regions is subject to your consent, which is requested within the App.
  • California (CCPA): We do not sell personal information. You may exercise your rights to know and to delete by contacting us below.
