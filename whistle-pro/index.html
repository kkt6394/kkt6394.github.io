Watch during the match, phone after it

Sunday leagues, clubs, youth leagues. The whole match runs on one watch — no phone needed.

⌚

Control without looking

Pause and resume, add a goal or undo with the watch buttons, crown or bezel while you run (PRO). On Apple Watch and Wear OS watches such as Galaxy Watch.

⏱️

Match timer and added time

Halves, quarters, extra time and penalties. Stoppages are timed separately to suggest added time.

📳

Vibration alerts

Your wrist buzzes just before the end, when half-time is over and when a sin-binned player can return.

⚽

Log in four taps

Record goals, cautions, sendings-off and substitutions with team and shirt number. A second caution becomes a sending-off automatically.

📋

Your league, your rules

Start from the FIFA standard, change match length, players, substitution limits and sin bins, and save it as a preset.

❤️

Workout data

Heart rate, distance, sprints and a movement heatmap are kept with the match record and saved as a workout to the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).

📤

Match report

When the match is over, share a report from your phone with goals, cards, substitutions and the rules applied.

🛰️

Line up the pitch

In the [Map] tab of match details, line up the four corners of the pitch to see your heatmap over a satellite photo.

🎬

15-second replay video

For matches with the pitch lined up, turn your movement into a 15-second replay video and post it straight to social media.

🎨

Team colors

Pick both teams' kit colors on the watch before kick-off, and fix them on your phone after the match.
